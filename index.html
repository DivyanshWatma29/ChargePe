The problem we’re exploring

Charging should feel like one journey.

Different operators can mean different apps, session flows, tariffs, and receipts. ChargePe is exploring a simpler front door—while being clear that interoperability depends on real operator access.

Interactive prototype

Scan the QR at the charging station.

In the intended journey, you arrive at a participating station and scan the QR label on the charger with ChargePe. The app identifies the station, shows the connector and estimated cost, then hands off to a supported payment flow.

Prototype today: try the camera scanner if your browser supports it, or enter a sample station code. Charging sessions and payments are simulated; no real charger or payment network is connected.

Proposed journey

From QR scan to a clearer receipt.

The long-term flow will work only for networks that make the appropriate station data, session controls, and payment methods available.

STEP 01

Discover a charger

Compare location, connector, reported status, and indicative pricing.

STEP 02

Scan station QR

Open the specific station and connector page. This prototype uses demo station IDs.

STEP 03

Review session details

See the estimated session cost before continuing. No money is collected here.

STEP 04

Record and reconcile

Plan for session history and fleet reports once trusted operator data is available.

Build in phases

Ambition first. Integrations when ready.

Start with a credible, testable interface. Keep the distinction between prototype features and future production capabilities explicit.

Phase 1 · Now

Discovery prototype

Static landing page, sample charger cards, QR-flow simulation, indicative costs, clear demo labels.

  • Free GitHub Pages hosting
  • Sample data only
  • No user account required
Phase 2 · Validate

Reliability and fleet tools

Test with drivers and fleet operators before locking the feature set.

  • Reports with timestamps
  • Session history and CSV
  • Fleet cost summaries
Phase 3 · Integrate

Participating networks

Requires operator agreements, technical access, security review, and payment-provider arrangements.

  • UBC/Beckn or OCPI, where available
  • Real start/stop and status
  • Supported payments and receipts
