Product thesis

A map is step one. Trust is the work.

Multi-network discovery and in-app payment already exist in the market. ChargePe’s initial hypothesis is to make status confidence, price expectations, and what happens after a failed session much more transparent.

QR → estimate → payment review

Start at the charger—not a wallet top-up.

The intended flow is to scan the QR label fixed to a participating charger, verify the station and connector, review an estimate, then use an authorised payment hand-off. Try a sample code below.

Built for a clear hand-off

ChargePe’s direction is to let drivers use a supported UPI app linked to their bank, without needing a separate ChargePe stored-value wallet. Real cross-network charging still requires the operator to authorise the session and a payment provider to handle collection and settlement.

For this prototype: QR scanning or sample-code entry identifies demo records. You can review an indicative cost and simulate the payment screen, but no payment occurs and no charger starts.

!Never enter your UPI PIN, bank password, card number or OTP into a prototype page. The live version here does not need any of those details.

AI assistant concept

Meet TARA.

TARA stands for Trip & Range Assistant. Ask in plain language to shortlist the cheapest sample charger, find a higher-power connector, compare report freshness, or estimate charging cost.

TARA · Trip & Range Assistant

Ask a charging question.

Instead of learning every filter, tell TARA what matters to you. It can explain its shortlist and show the data used for the estimate.

Current mode: local rule-based matching over six sample station records. Claude mode can be enabled after the serverless Worker is deployed, its API secret is configured, and TARA_API_URL is set. Neither mode has live charger telemetry or GPS.

Future AI direction: range-aware trip planning, tariff explanations, invoice anomaly flags, and evidence-based alternatives. These are planned ideas, not implemented services.

Roadmap

Prototype honestly. Integrate deliberately.

ChargePe is an early-stage product thesis, not a live charging network. Each production feature depends on trusted data, user validation, operator participation and appropriate payment/security controls.

Now · Browser prototype

Explore the flow

Interactive station map, filterable illustrative directory, QR/code identification, cost estimate, payment simulation and TARA rule-based chat.

  • Static site hosting
  • No account or install
  • No real payments
Next · Validate

Prove the trust layer

Talk to drivers and fleet operators; test what makes them trust or avoid a station listing.

  • Source and timestamp every status
  • Capture structured failure reports
  • Test estimates and receipt UX
Later · Integrations

Connect real networks

Requires operator participation, API access, a backend, security reviews and a supported payment provider.

  • Station/operator integrations
  • Authorised charging controls
  • UPI flow and reconciled receipts

The initial wedge: reliability clarity, not another map.

Existing Indian products already offer combinations of multi-network discovery, availability, QR flows and payments. ChargePe will test whether clearly sourced status confidence, transparent cost expectations and consistent session records solve an important unmet problem. This is a hypothesis to validate with users—not a claim that competitors lack these features.

Help shape the next iteration.

ChargePe is an early-stage open-source project. Feedback from EV drivers, fleet managers and charging operators can help validate the product thesis before integrations are built.
